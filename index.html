Pub Call

Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Pub Call lets you and your mates see who's at which pub, and give a heads-up when you're heading out. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. Pub Call is for people aged 18 and over.

The short version

  • Your exact location never leaves your phone. Friends only see the name of the pub you choose to share.
  • Only people you've added as mates can see anything about you, and you can narrow that down to groups or particular people.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking across other apps, and nothing is ever sold.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

What stays on your phone

  • Your location. Pub Call uses your location on your phone to notice when you've been at a pub for about 5 minutes. Your coordinates are never sent to us or anyone else. You can turn this off under Me → Detect pubs automatically and check in by hand instead.
  • Your home suburb, and app settings such as ghost mode.

What's stored on our server

  • Your email address, so you can sign in with an emailed code.
  • Your profile: display name, username, colour and avatar symbol. Anyone who knows your username can send you a request to be mates; they see your display name, but nothing else until you accept.
  • Your mates (people who've accepted your request, or whose request you accepted), requests you've sent and received, and groups you make. Only you can see your groups.
  • Check-ins: the pub, when you arrived, when you said you'd leave (if you did), and who can see it. A check-in ends when you leave, check out, or after 6 hours.
  • Calls ("Seb is going to Beach Road"): the pub, its suburb, the mates you tagged, an optional note, and who can see it. Calls disappear from the app when the time you gave is up, and never last more than 12 hours. Friends who tap "I might come down" are recorded on that call.
  • Pub crawls you plan or join: the name, the pubs and their order, where the crawl is up to, who was invited and who's in (with their display name and avatar, which others on the same crawl can see even if they aren't your mates). While a crawl you're in is at a pub, the people who can see the crawl see you as there.
  • Ratings and notes you give pubs. Your mates see your rating and note. Your rating is also counted in a pub's overall average, which everyone can see once a pub has at least two ratings; the average never shows who rated it, and notes are only ever shown to mates.
  • Notification settings: who you've muted, your quiet hours and your time zone.
  • A push notification token for your device, if notifications are on.
  • Reports and blocks. If you report someone, a call or a rating note, we store who reported what, why, and when, so we can look into it. The person isn't told who reported them. If you block someone, we store that so they can't add you back.

Who can see it

Only your mates, and only what you share with them. People you're not mates with can't see your profile, check-ins, calls, or individual ratings and notes (only an anonymous pub average, as above). If you tag a mate in a call, your other mates who can see that call will see their display name. Mutes and quiet hours are never shown to anyone.

Reporting and blocking

You can report a mate, one of their calls, or a rating note, and block anyone you're mates with, from their page or the call (tap ⋯). Blocking removes them as a mate straight away and stops them adding you again. We review reports and remove content or accounts that break the rules: no abuse, harassment, hate or spam.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores the data above and handles sign-in.
  • Google (Gmail) sends sign-in code emails.
  • Apple Push Notification service delivers notifications.

These services only process data to run Pub Call.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept while you have an account. Expired check-ins and calls are hidden from everyone. Reports are kept for as long as we need them to deal with the problem. When you delete your account, everything listed above is deleted straight away, including your name on mates' calls. Copies may remain in the hosting provider's routine backups for a short time before they're overwritten.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Me → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, profile, mates, groups, check-ins, calls, pub crawls you planned or joined, ratings, blocks, reports you've made and settings. It can't be undone.

Your rights

You can see and change your profile in the app, and delete everything by deleting your account. For any other request about your information, or a question about this policy, email pubcall.contact@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on this page, with a new date at the top.
